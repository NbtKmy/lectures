The hour

  • Introduction: 5 min
  • Task 1, hands-on: 25 min
  • Task 2, tour: 15 min
  • Task 3, slides: 7 min
  • Limits: 6 min
  • Wrap-up: 2 min

What you need

  • QGIS 3.44 is recommended; other versions work, but menus differ slightly.
  • Two data files for Task 1: boundaries and residents table.
  • An internet connection for the base map.
  • Task 2 and 3 files are in the repository folder.

Where the data come from

  • Boundaries (N03) and hospitals (P04): MLIT National Land Numerical Information, P04.
  • Population grid: 2020 Census, e-Stat.
  • Foreign residents: Statistics on Foreign Residents, e-Stat, December 2025.
  • Roads and walking: OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Base map: GSI tiles. Municipality codes: Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications.
