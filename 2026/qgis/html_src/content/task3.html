Task 3 · Walking access across Moriyama Ward

Question: of all the people living in Moriyama Ward, what share can walk to at least one hospital in 10, 20 or 30 minutes? About 7 minutes, slides only — you will not repeat these steps today.

The result

10 min31,287.8017.90%
20 min81,492.4146.61%
30 min109,452.9362.60%

All shares are out of 174,833.87 estimated residents of the ward. Numbers are estimates, so the decimals are not meant to be exact.

Four steps

  1. Reach of each hospital. For each of 41 hospitals in or within 3 km of the ward, find the roads reachable on foot (4 km/h) within 10, 20 and 30 minutes. Draw a 30 m buffer around those roads.
  2. Dissolve. For each time, merge the 41 buffers into one shape. A person who lives near two hospitals is counted once.
  3. Clip. Cut the merged shape to the ward boundary.
  4. Count people. Use area apportioning on the 250 m population grid: people inside the shape divided by all people in the ward.

Why Dissolve matters. Adding up the people near each hospital would count some residents several times. Merging first removes the overlaps.

Approximate, potential access. These are estimates of who could walk to a hospital, based on roads and a buffer. They do not show who actually goes there, or whether a hospital can treat them. 62.60% is a result of this method, not a verified fact.

This reach was built with a different method from Task 2 (a buffer around reachable roads instead of a smoothed walking-time surface), so do not compare the two numbers.
